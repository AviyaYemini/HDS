Build the foundation of HDS 2.0 from docs/hds-spec.md sections 2, 4 (AUTH-* and ORG-* rows) and docs/hds-api.md (Auth and Organisation).

Users sign in and see what their role allows. Roles: Employee, Manager (company-wide view, inherits employee actions), Admin (a manager who can also delete employees and projects), Client (own projects only). Managers and admins maintain employees, groups, qualifications, clients, sites and projects (a project has a type, a start date and a billing rate). Employees can view and edit only their own profile.

Out of scope for this feature: availability, shifts, scheduling, money reports, mobile. Do not mention any technology.
