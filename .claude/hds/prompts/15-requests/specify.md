Employees ask for time off, swaps and open shifts through the app, and managers decide in one place. Use docs/hds-spec.md rows AVL-4 and SCH-6 and idea IDEA-1, and docs/research/ux-improvements-2026-11.md rows E5, E14, S20 and NT8. Roles allowed: employee (requests), manager and admin (decisions). Business rules from the original: absence types are leave, reserve duty and sick. Proposed (owner decision O3): an absence is a request with approval, by a manager of a project the employee works on or an admin; an approved absence blocks scheduling; the employee stays responsible for a shift until a swap is approved; offers go only to qualified, eligible people. No technology details. Out of scope: pay effects of absences (feature 017).
