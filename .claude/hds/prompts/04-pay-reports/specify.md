Admins set each employee's default pay rate and can override pay on a single shift; an employee sees a monthly pay forecast from scheduled shifts, and managers see work-log and hours reports. Use docs/hds-spec.md (FIN-1, FIN-3, RPT-1, RPT-2) and original scenarios E and F.

Effective pay for a shift is the shift's own rate if set, otherwise the employee's default. Rates and times must be positive. Employees see only their own pay; managers see hours but pay only where permitted by role. Work-log report: who worked at which project, defaulting the start to the project start date.

No technology details.
